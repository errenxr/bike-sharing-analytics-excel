# Business Questions

## Project Background

Bike-sharing services generate data that can help businesses understand rental demand, customer behavior, and usage patterns over time.

By analyzing historical bike rental data, businesses can identify periods of high demand, understand differences in rental behavior between customer segments, and recognize seasonal trends.

This project analyzes the Bike Sharing Dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository, covering rental records from 2011 to 2012. The analysis is conducted using Microsoft Excel to extract meaningful insights and support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Understand hourly bike rental demand and identify peak rental hours.
- Compare rental patterns between working days and non-working days.
- Analyze differences in rental behavior between casual and registered users.
- Identify seasonal and monthly rental trends.
- Examine rental distribution across different periods of the day.
- Develop actionable business recommendations based on the findings.

## Key Business Questions

### 1. Hourly Rental Demand Analysis

How does bike rental demand vary by hour and working day throughout 2011–2012, and during which hours should the company increase bike availability to improve service efficiency?

### 2. Customer Segment Analysis

How do rental patterns differ between casual and registered users across seasons, and which customer segment should be prioritized in marketing strategies to increase bike rentals?

### 3. Monthly Rental Trend Analysis

How does bike rental demand change across months and years, and are there any noticeable trends or seasonal fluctuations?

### 4. Time Period Analysis

How is bike rental demand distributed across different periods of the day, and which time period contributes the most to total rentals?

## Expected Outcomes

Through these analyses, the project aims to:

- Identify peak rental hours and periods of high demand.
- Understand differences in rental activity between working and non-working days.
- Evaluate the contribution of casual and registered user segments.
- Identify seasonal and monthly demand patterns.
- Provide business recommendations for operational planning, customer acquisition, and customer retention.
